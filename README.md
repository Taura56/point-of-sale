# Point of Sale & Business Manager

A business management and point-of-sale application designed to help small and independent businesses manage their daily operations.

## 🚀 Live Demo

[Open Point of Sale](https://point-of-sale-1-tghr.onrender.com)

## ✨ Features

- **Dashboard:** View important business performance figures.
- **Cyber Services:** Manage services and record customer transactions.
- **Sales Management:** Record product sales and track sales activity.
- **Repair Management:** Manage repair jobs and monitor their progress.
- **Inventory Management:** Keep track of business products and stock.
- **Expense Management:** Record and monitor business expenses.
- **Reports:** Review business performance and financial activity.

## 🛠️ Technology Stack

- **Frontend:** Flutter
- **Backend:** Node.js, Express.js
- **Database:** MongoDB
- **Deployment:** Render

## 🎯 Project Goal

The goal of this project is to provide small business owners with a practical digital tool for managing sales, services, inventory, expenses, repairs, and business performance in one place.
